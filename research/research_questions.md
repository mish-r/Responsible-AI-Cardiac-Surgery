# Research Questions

1. How are LLMs, VLMs and multimodal AI being used in cardiac surgery?

2. What are the main reliability and safety challenges of generative AI in cardiac-surgical settings?

3. Can Retrieval-Augmented Generation improve the accuracy and evidence-based nature of AI-generated responses?

4. How can hallucinations, uncertainty and potential bias be identified and reduced?

5. What role should human oversight play when generative AI is used to support cardiac-surgical workflows?
