# Research Direction

## Working Research Title

Responsible and Trustworthy Generative AI in Cardiac Surgery.

## Background

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being explored in healthcare, including cardiovascular medicine and cardiac surgery.

Newer technologies such as Large Language Models, Vision-Language Models and multimodal AI offer opportunities to process clinical information and support healthcare professionals.

However, these systems can produce incorrect information, demonstrate bias or provide unreliable outputs.

## Research Focus

This research will explore:

- LLMs and VLMs in cardiac surgery.
- Multimodal and hybrid AI systems.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
- Reliability and hallucinations.
- Bias, fairness and AI ethics.
- Human oversight and clinical safety.

## Research Aim

To investigate how generative AI systems can be evaluated and used responsibly and reliably in cardiac-surgical settings.

## Potential Research Gap

There may be a need for more research evaluating the reliability, safety and clinical oversight of generative AI in cardiac surgery, particularly regarding hallucinations, evidence verification and multimodal information.

This potential gap will be examined through the literature review.
