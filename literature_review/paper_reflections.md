# Literature Review – Paper Reflections

## Paper 13

**Patel et al. (2026)**

*Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Global Cardiac Surgery: A Scoping Review*

### About
- Reviews 83 studies about AI and machine learning in cardiac surgery.
- Focuses on predicting complications and patient outcomes.

### Interesting Findings
- 74.7% of studies came from China.
- Raises questions about bias and whether AI works equally well across populations.

### Limitations
- Most studies focused on traditional AI rather than generative AI.
- Limited research from different countries.
- Some models lacked external validation.

### Relevance to My Research
- My research focuses on newer AI technologies, particularly LLMs and VLMs.
- Interested in hallucinations, reliability and patient safety.

### Reflection
I liked this paper because it directly relates to cardiac surgery. It helped me understand existing AI applications and what could be explored further.

**Main takeaway:** How can newer AI technologies be made safer and more trustworthy in cardiac surgery?
