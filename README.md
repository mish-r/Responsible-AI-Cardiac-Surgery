# Responsible AI in Cardiac Surgery

## About the Research

This repository documents my PhD research into the responsible and trustworthy use of Generative Artificial Intelligence in cardiac surgery.

The research explores how newer AI technologies could support healthcare while considering their safety, reliability and limitations.

## Research Focus

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- Multimodal and Hybrid AI
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Agentic AI
- AI ethics, bias and fairness
- Hallucination detection and reliability
- Human oversight and accountability

## Research Areas

The research focuses on:
- AI applications involving the heart and cardiovascular system.
- AI applications in cardiac and cardiothoracic surgery.
- Responsible and trustworthy AI in healthcare.
- Safety and reliability of generative AI systems.

## Literature Review

The literature review investigates existing AI research in cardiac surgery and explores potential research gaps involving generative AI.

See the `literature_review` folder for search keywords, Boolean expressions and paper reflections.

## Technical Development

An existing PDF text-mining and stylometric analysis workflow is being explored for use with cardiac-surgery literature.

The current workflow involves:
1. Selecting and downloading academic papers manually.
2. Uploading PDFs to Google Drive.
3. Automatically processing and analysing the extracted text.

## Repository Structure

- `research/` – Research direction and questions.
- `literature_review/` – Search strategy and paper reflections.
- `automation/` – Documentation of the automation workflow.
- `progress/` – Research progress summaries.
- `references/` – Academic references.

## Current Status

This repository is being developed alongside the PhD research. Literature collection, research development and technical testing are ongoing.
